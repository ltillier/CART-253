### Week 1:
I started this class with no clue about anything we were going to do and no idea what to expect. I had trouble setting up the necessary software and getting started, but I think I got the hang of things pretty quickly after asking (and getting!) help. I think the main thing that surprised me was how simple/intuitive the challenges/assignments were, since I was honestly anticipating that I would struggle a lot. A big part of that is probably due to the way the instructions are given (they're very easy to follow), but I'm also a little surprised at how much fun I had. I didn't do much in class, but as soon as I got home I began to fiddle around with my website. I think what I've struggled with the most so far has been asking for help and committing to do things even if I'm not sure if I'm doing them right but I'm not surprised since that's something I tend to worry about in general. I've been somewhat worried about my commit messages since a part of me wonders if I'm leaving too much information in the message portion (I tend to overuse parentheses and forward slashes when left to my own devices). I feel like there are a lot of directions I can go with this course (I also feel like I don't have a super clear idea of what I'm actually meant to end up with, in a way?). I want to be able to make something helpful for people but I also kind of want to make something that is somewhat odd, very useless, and fun.

I missed class this week because I slept until 5pm (post all-nighter misery (I genuinely felt hunted so I couldn't sleep until past 2am)) and thought, man, I do NOT want to go to school for 4 hours. In hindsight, this was a crazy and insane miscalculation, as me not doing the instructions challenge until Friday snowballed into me not doing the prototypes, which snowballed into me not doing anything. I guess. So that's irritating. Through the process of making these prototypes, the main thing on my mind was that I just wasn't sure what I should actually aim to make. I like drawing and generally am able to scribble whatever, wherever, whenever (I carry notebooks on me for this reason). Drawing with functions is very different, and I kept running into issues where if I went in with a very clear idea of what I wanted to make, I would fail to meet my own expectations, and if I went in with no idea what to make, I would struggle to come up with a concrete plan, since the possibilities are endless and all equally unfamiliar to me. I did have fun, though. The biggest issue I had/am having is that I keep missing small (but vital!) spelling/grammar mistakes that result in nothing working. So after meeting with Sabine and doing the challenge, I was super excited to get home and start working on prototypes, but genuinely nothing I did was working and I had no idea why. I come to the next class with nothing, and I complain briefly to Tiny, who immediately pointed out that I hadn't used pascalCase for the createCanvas function. So. I'm being a lot more thorough now. Also small bright side, when I missed class Tiny messaged me almost immediately to ask if I had dropped it, and having people notice my presence and absense means a lot! I do love making friends.
